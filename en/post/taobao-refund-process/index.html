Taobao Return Guide: Tips for Refunds & Returns

What one Taobao return actually looked like: talking the seller round, picking the right return reason, shipping back to China with Taiwan Post e-Packet, and getting the shipping fee back out of official support.

Introduction

Taobao offers a wide range of products at attractive prices. Many items, including charging cables, power banks, and insulated bottles, often cost less than similar products sold in Taiwan. Given that these items are often manufactured in China and some falsely labeled as "Made in Taiwan" despite being imported, buying directly from Taobao can sometimes offer better value.

However, my recent experience revealed some frustrating pitfalls. I bought an item from Seller B because it was cheaper than Seller A, but when it arrived, I was shocked to find that the product looked nothing like the online description and was of terrible quality.

Even though the item was only CNY 47.7 (USD 6.58), receiving such an obvious counterfeit left me feeling frustrated, and I immediately decided to start the return process.

Other buyers have shared their experiences too: Appendix: Return Case Sharing

Communicating with the Original Seller

Upon realizing the item was counterfeit, I contacted the seller. However, the process was far from smooth. Despite clearly explaining the discrepancy, I received canned responses like:

"I'm still young and can't understand pictures, so please describe the problem simply again. Thank you!"

The seller insisted that I choose "wrong specification selected" as the return reason and ship via a specific logistics provider that offers pickup services. Unfortunately, such services are unavailable in Taiwan, complicating the return process further.

If faced with such uncooperative sellers, contacting Taobao's official customer support often resolves the issue.

Taobao's Dedicated Support for Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macau, and Overseas

Taobao's customer support for Hong Kong, Macau, Taiwan, and other overseas regions picks up fast. Once you give them the order details and explain the issue, they answer as quickly as they can. Key points to note:

  1. Provide the order number in every interaction with customer support.
  2. Clearly discuss who is responsible for the return shipping costs.
  3. When shipping from Taiwan to China, use the postal service and obtain a tracking number. Enter this tracking number into the Taobao return system under the logistics option "China Post." You can track the shipment via China Post.
  4. If you have good Sesame Credit, Taobao may refund your payment immediately after submitting the return request.

Taobao Return Process

Requesting a Return

After selecting the product in the system, click on "Return" and negotiate with the seller. If any uncertainties arise, please communicate with the seller first to confirm whether the product needs to be reshipped or returned directly. If the product has defects, make sure to select options such as "Quality issue" or "Item not as described," and provide sufficient evidence to demonstrate any faults or issues with the product.

⚠️ If the product has defects, some sellers may try to guide you towards options like "Incorrect size" or "Changed my mind." Please disregard these suggestions, as they may mislead the processing of return shipping fees, especially when the product has defects or does not match the description.

After submitting, check that the product really has entered the return process. If return shipping fees are required, negotiate with the seller. If the seller refuses to refund the shipping fee, and you suspect the product is counterfeit, take it to Taobao's official customer service.

Shipping the Product Back

At first, I wasn't sure whether to use SF Express or the post office. In the end, I went with the post office because the process is simpler, there are plenty of locations, and it's just more convenient overall. Since my item was small, I decided to drop it off in person.

Keep in mind that items must not be left exposed. Make sure to pack them securely in a box with some waterproofing. If your item is fragile or easily damaged, add some padding to protect it during shipping.

This was my first time shipping internationally, so I wasn't totally familiar with the process. Luckily, the staff were friendly and helpful. Once I explained that this was a personal package going to Mainland China, they walked me through my options and suggested the right outer packaging. As long as your package isn't too big, you can usually get everything wrapped up and shipped on the spot.

Shipping Procedure:

  1. Complete the Postal e-Packet form at the post office and inform the staff that you require a registered package with recipient confirmation.
  2. After checking, the staff will remind you that the item needs to be properly packaged. They'll recommend buying a standard convenience bag for simple packaging.
  3. The convenience bag cost is deducted from the postage fee. In my case, the total postage was TWD 150, and the TWD 65 bag cost was deducted, so the actual postage came out to TWD 85. But you still pay TWD 150 total, which means the bag is basically free.

Before you ship, double-check with the staff that you're sending it as registered mail (which requires the recipient to sign for it) and not as regular mail. This helps avoid tracking or return issues down the line. You'll also need to fill in the complete sender and recipient details.

After shipping, the post office will issue a shipping receipt. Photograph it right away and upload the image to Taobao as proof for the return and refund process. The receipt's yellow tracking number should be entered in the Taobao return form.

Within a few days, you can check the shipping status via China Postal Express & Logistics by entering the tracking number to view the current location of your package. Remember to take screenshots for documentation to prevent any disputes regarding non-receipt.

For detailed postage fee calculations for cross-strait Postal e-Packet shipments, please refer to the Taiwan Post guidelines:

  1. Fees are calculated per 0.5 kg; items weighing less than 0.5 kg are charged as 0.5 kg.
  2. Charges are based on the greater of actual weight or volumetric weight: (Length × Width × Height in cm) ÷ 6000 = Volumetric Weight (kg).
  3. If the volumetric weight exceeds 2 kg, an additional fee of TWD 20 per 0.5 kg is applied, regardless of the destination zone.
  4. Each package must not exceed 2 kg.
  5. No side may exceed 60 cm, and the sum of length, width, and height must not exceed 90 cm. The minimum dimensions are 14 cm × 9 cm.
  6. The weight and pricing in TWD are as follows: Zone 1 comprises Guangdong, Guangxi, Hainan, Fujian, Jiangxi, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and Shanghai; all other regions are classified as Zone 2.

Filling in the Taobao Return Tracking Number and Follow-up

Once you have the shipping receipt, enter its tracking number into Taobao's return system. The return isn't officially filed until you do. If you have a favorable Sesame Credit rating, the refund is issued the moment you submit.

Once you've completed the return registration, keep an eye on whether the seller has received the package. If any issues come up, reach out to Taobao's official customer service right away.

If the return deadline is coming up and Taobao's system can't track your package, don't wait. Contact customer service proactively. Give them the China Postal Express & Logistics tracking results and the package tracking number, along with any other relevant info, so they can help track things down.

The return isn't over until the system shows "Refund Successful" and the money is actually back.

Shipping Fee Refund

Once the product is successfully returned to the seller and the system displays "Refund Successful", the return process is essentially complete.

Because the product did not match its description, I applied for a shipping fee refund through Taobao's official customer service. After negotiations with the seller proved unsuccessful, I contacted Taobao's dedicated customer support again and clearly stated my request: "I want a shipping fee refund."

Taobao customer service required me to provide the following details:

  1. Tracking number.
  2. Logistics company: China Post Express Logistics (shipped from Taiwan and forwarded to China Post Express Logistics).
  3. Shipping fee: TWD 150 (CNY 33.30).
  4. Seller's return address.
  5. Contact email.
  6. Contact phone number (I provided my Taiwan mobile number).

Finally, they asked if I would accept compensation via a red packet (a Taobao store credit or bonus that can be used for future purchases), and I agreed.

I've read online that some users had trouble using red packets because they hadn't completed real-name verification. If this applies to you, it's worth confirming with Taobao ahead of time.

After submitting the request, the customer service representative indicated they would respond within 3 to 5 business days. However, no progress was made even after the 5-day period.

During this time, my schedule was fragmented due to travel, and I did not actively follow up with them.

In early November, I contacted Taobao again to report that I had not received the red packet. It seemed that the process was restarted, causing further delays. Without actively following up, the issue dragged on for some time.

Here's the key takeaway: keep a close eye on the progress during the promised timeframe (say, 3 days). If you don't hear back by then, reach out to Taobao customer service right away. In my experience, they only start taking action after you follow up multiple times. Don't wait too long—if they promise a response in 3 days and you wait 7 days to follow up, they might not take responsibility anymore.

The red packet compensation eventually landed on November 13, with no usage restrictions.

Appendix: Return Case Sharing

Case 1: Handling Return Requests Beyond 7 Days

A reader bought a CNY 69 item and only noticed the fault about a month in. The order was well past the 7-day window, so the Taobao app wouldn't take a return request at all and the only route left was the seller's after-sales service.

  1. Contacting the Seller

    • They first went to customer service through the product's own app, which told them to deal with the Taobao seller directly.
    • The seller asked for the item to be returned to a designated address, with the order number and fault description inside the package.
    • The seller covered domestic shipping within China, but international shipping was on the buyer.
    • International shipping cost more than the item itself, so it was worth going to Taobao's official customer service and settling who pays before shipping anything.

  2. Contacting Taobao's Official Customer Service

    • They typed "I want a human agent" in the Taobao app and selected the relevant order.
    • The first replies were the standard canned ones, so they pushed back on the international shipping cost.
    • Taobao took their account history into consideration and approved a "Customer Experience Guarantee," issuing a full refund of CNY 69, credited to a Taobao red envelope (store credit) within three working days.

This case shows that Taobao sellers usually cover domestic return shipping, but international shipping is on you and you have to raise it with official customer service yourself. Follow up a few times and the full refund does come through.

Case 2: Product Quality Issue

I bought a product with a lot of good reviews, and the seller's showcase had a fair amount of real feedback on it too. It broke after less than a day of use.

I immediately contacted the seller, who acknowledged the defect and agreed to resend a new product.

After confirming with Taobao's official customer service, I was informed:

The new package tracking number for the reshipped item cannot be uploaded to the original order. Please inform the seller that the corresponding warehouse address and identification code must match the original order's information before shipping to avoid being rejected by the warehouse.

Friendly reminder: As the direct shipping address code has a validity period, it is recommended to reship the item as soon as possible.

I passed this information to the seller and received the replacement product a few days later without having to pay any additional shipping fees.

If the seller is willing to resend the product, it might be easier to accept this solution.

Case 3: Local Return in Taiwan

I purchased another product that had a quality defect and could not function properly after charging. The product supports local returns in Taiwan, so after negotiating with the seller, the seller asked me to keep the machine and refunded me CNY 5. But what am I supposed to keep if the product itself is defective?

Since the product supports local returns in Taiwan, I proceeded with the return. The first time I used Taiwan's local return, the shipping fee was zero. If I have to bear the shipping cost in the future, I can negotiate with Taobao's official customer service to claim compensation for the shipping fee due to the product defect.

After applying for a local return in Taiwan, I had the option to choose between convenience store pickup or home delivery for pickup. I packed the product and chose home delivery for pickup. Two days later, the delivery company completed the pickup, and the product was returned to the seller a few days later. Once the product was picked up by the delivery company, I did not need to intervene further. After the product was returned to the seller, the refund was automatically credited to my account.

⚠️ Important Warning: Scenarios Where Taiwan Local Return May Not Be Available

Based on feedback shared by reader vw1298, even if a product is labeled as supporting "TW Local Return" and you have purchased premium services, the convenience store return option may still be unavailable due to the following conditions:

  1. Some items in the same package have already used the cross-border return service
  2. The selected return reason is "Wrong Item Shipped" or "Counterfeit Product"
  3. The package has not yet been dispatched from the warehouse when you apply for a return
  4. The order involves multiple packages (e.g., the seller ships items in separate batches)
  5. Cainiao logistics system determines the package cannot be collected

These conditions may prevent the system from activating the "Taiwan Local Return" mechanism. In such cases, you will not be able to use the convenience store return service and must arrange return shipping through carriers like SF Express, EMS, FedEx, or DHL yourself. The shipping cost may far exceed the value of the product.

Recommendations:

  • Before purchasing, confirm that the product will be shipped in a single package. Avoid sellers who may ship items in multiple batches.
  • Avoid products with unusually low prices or minimal sales, as these carry higher risks.
  • If local return is unavailable, you may try reporting the discrepancy between the listing and actual service to Taobao customer service, but the final decision rests with the platform.

⭐ Go for products that support local returns in Taiwan whenever you can. It saves a lot of unnecessary hassle. ⭐

Conclusion

Taobao products are often a case of "you get what you pay for." Besides the product description on the page, always check buyer reviews and photos, which reflect the actual product far better. If no one has bought the product or left a review, skip that seller.

If you have any doubts about a product, favor items labeled "Supports Local Return in Taiwan (TW Local Return)." If you receive an unsatisfactory product, you can apply for a return within 15 days of the delivery date (based on the logistics receipt timestamp). Someone comes to collect it, and you never have to deal with the recipient's address or anything else.

The return process itself is fine. Shipping fees are the hard part. If the seller won't cover return shipping, go straight to official customer service. Shipping an item back from Taiwan often costs more than the item did, so settle that with customer service before you commit to returning anything.
