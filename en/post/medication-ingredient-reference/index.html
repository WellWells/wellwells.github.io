Introduction

When traveling to Japan, buying pharmaceutical and cosmetic products as souvenirs or for proxy shopping has become the norm, mainly because they work fast and are easy to take.

Despite Taiwan's comprehensive National Health Insurance system—where consultation fees typically range from TWD 180 to 250—many households prefer self-administering over-the-counter medications such as Taisho Pabron Gold A or Panadol to relieve mild cold symptoms, including fever and nasal congestion. Analgesics like EVE are commonly chosen by women as convenient, immediate solutions for headaches and menstrual pain.

But certain active ingredients in medications such as EVE are explicitly prohibited from being imported or used in South Korea. Travelers who don't know what's in the box risk detention or fines at customs. Knowing which ingredients sit in the common products is what keeps that from happening.

Combination Cold Remedies

Common Ingredients in Cold Medications

  1. Antipyretic Analgesics

    • Acetaminophen / アセトアミノフェン: Inhibits central prostaglandin synthesis to reduce fever and pain; adverse effects include hepatotoxicity at excessive doses and potential renal impairment with prolonged high‑dose use.
    • Ibuprofen / イブプロフェン: A non‑steroidal anti‑inflammatory drug (NSAID) that suppresses cyclooxygenase activity to provide antipyretic, analgesic, and anti‑inflammatory effects; common adverse reactions include gastrointestinal discomfort, increased risk of gastric ulceration, and renal burden.

  2. Central Cough Suppressants

    • Dihydrocodeine Phosphate / ジヒドロコデインリン酸塩: Acts on the medullary cough center to suppress cough reflex; adverse effects include dependence, respiratory depression, constipation, and sedation.
    • Dextromethorphan Hydrobromide Hydrate / デキストロメトルファン臭化水素酸塩水和物: Central antitussive that inhibits the cough center; adverse effects include dizziness and nausea, with potential neuropsychiatric symptoms in overdose.
    • Noscapine / ノスカピン: Non‑addictive central cough suppressant suitable for dry or nocturnal cough; adverse effects include nausea, dizziness, and rare allergic reactions.

  3. Expectorants

    • Guaifenesin / グアイフェネシン: Reduces sputum viscosity and promotes bronchial secretion clearance; adverse effects may include nausea, headache, and rash.
    • Guaiacol Potassium Sulfonate / グアヤコールスルホン酸カリウム: Facilitates expectoration by diluting bronchial secretions; adverse effects include gastrointestinal discomfort and headache.
    • Bromhexine Hydrochloride / ブロムヘキシン塩酸塩: Breaks down mucopolysaccharide structure to enhance ciliary motility; adverse effects include gastrointestinal discomfort and rash.
    • Ambroxol Hydrochloride / アンブロキソール塩酸塩: Promotes ciliary activity and mucolysis to improve sputum clearance; adverse effects include gastrointestinal discomfort and rare skin allergy.
    • Glycyrrhiza Extract / カンゾウエキス: Exhibits anti‑inflammatory and expectorant properties; long‑term high‑dose use may cause edema and hypertension.

  4. Bronchodilators

    • dl‑Methylephedrine Hydrochloride / dl‑メチルエフェドリン塩酸塩: Relaxes bronchial smooth muscle and stimulates the sympathetic nervous system to relieve cough and nasal congestion; adverse effects include palpitations, hypertension, and insomnia.

  5. Antihistamines

    • Chlorpheniramine Maleate / クロルフェニラミンマレイン酸塩: First‑generation antihistamine for relief of sneezing, rhinorrhea, and nasal congestion; adverse effects include drowsiness, dry mouth, and dizziness.
    • Clemastine Fumarate / クレマスチンフマル酸塩: First‑generation antihistamine indicated for allergic rhinitis and urticaria; adverse effects include drowsiness, dry mouth, and blurred vision.

  6. Anticholinergics

    • Belladonna Total Alkaloids / ベラドンナ総アルカロイド: Exhibits anticholinergic action to relax smooth muscle, relieving nasal congestion and gastrointestinal spasms; adverse effects include dry mouth, tachycardia, blurred vision, and urinary retention.
    • Isopropamide Iodide / ヨウ化イソプロパミド: Inhibits glandular secretion to reduce rhinorrhea; adverse effects include dry mouth, blurred vision, and palpitations.

  7. Antifibrinolytics / Hemostatics

    • Tranexamic Acid / トラネキサム酸: Inhibits plasminogen activation to provide anti‑inflammatory and hemostatic effects, used to relieve sore throat swelling; adverse effects include nausea, vomiting, and rare thrombotic risk.

  8. Analgesic Adjuvants

    • Anhydrous Caffeine / 無水カフェイン: Inhibits cerebral vasodilation to assist in migraine relief and enhances analgesic efficacy while providing mild CNS stimulation.

  9. Other Traditional Ingredients

    • Kakkonto Extract / 葛根湯エキス: A traditional Kampo formula promoting diaphoresis and antipyresis to relieve neck and shoulder stiffness; adverse effects include gastrointestinal discomfort and skin allergy.

Throat / Cough Medication

Common Ingredients in Throat and Cough Medications

  1. Cough Suppressant / Expectorants

    • Platycodon Powder: Has cough-suppressing and expectorant properties, and helps treat respiratory-related diseases such as pharyngitis and abscess.
    • Senega officinalis Powder: Promotes the expulsion of phlegm and alleviates respiratory discomfort, aligning with its expectorant function.
    • Glycyrrhiza Powder: Suppresses cough, expels phlegm, and has anti-inflammatory and gastric mucosal protective effects. It is commonly used in Traditional Chinese Medicine to treat respiratory diseases.
    • Apricot Kernel Powder: Bitter almond extract in northern apricot seeds has cough-suppressing and expectorant properties but should be used cautiously due to its toxicity. Southern apricot is milder and more focused on moisturizing the lungs and stopping coughing.
    • Ginseng Powder: Possesses anti-inflammatory and immune-regulating effects, helping to alleviate coughing and repair throat mucosa.
    • Powdered Gambir: Has mild anti-inflammatory properties, protects the throat mucosa, and promotes self-cleansing functions.

  2. Anti-inflammatory / Hemostatic Agents

    • Tranexamic Acid: Inhibits fibrinolytic enzyme activity, with anti-inflammatory and hemostatic effects, reducing throat swelling and pain.
    • Azulene Sulfonate Sodium Hydrate: Inhibits the release of inflammatory mediators, has anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties, and alleviates throat mucosal swelling and pain.

  3. Antimicrobial / Antiseptics

    • Iodine: Has broad-spectrum antimicrobial effects, quickly sterilizing and killing viruses, but should be used cautiously in children, as it may temporarily affect thyroid function, especially in premature infants or children with immature thyroid glands.
    • Povidone-Iodine: Releases free iodine ions continuously, providing long-lasting antimicrobial effects with relatively low irritation. Though safer for infants in lower concentrations, caution should still be taken regarding potential thyroid effects in newborns and premature infants.
    • Cetylpyridinium Chloride Hydrate: Reduces the local pathogen load by disrupting pathogen cell membranes and acts quickly on viruses. It is safe at appropriate concentrations (usually not exceeding 1000 μg/mL), but overuse should be avoided.

Painkillers

Common Painkiller Ingredients

  1. Antipyretic Analgesics

    • アセトアミノフェン / Acetaminophen: Inhibits the synthesis of prostaglandins in the central nervous system to achieve antipyretic and analgesic effects; side effects include liver toxicity risk from overdose, and prolonged high doses may cause kidney damage.
    • イブプロフェン / Ibuprofen: A nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug that inhibits enzyme activity in the body responsible for inflammation and pain, thereby reducing prostaglandin production. It has antipyretic, analgesic, and anti-inflammatory effects. Common side effects include gastrointestinal discomfort, increased risk of ulcers, and kidney strain.

  2. Analgesic Adjuvants

    • アリルイソプロピルアセチル尿素 / Allylisopropylacetylurea: Has a sedative synergistic effect and can enhance the analgesic effect of ibuprofen by inhibiting cerebral vasodilation; may cause significant drowsiness, so driving or operating machinery should be avoided after use. Recommended for patients 15 years and older; not recommended for children or pregnant women.

  3. Adjuvant Pain-Relieving Ingredients

    • 無水カフェイン / Anhydrous Caffeine: Inhibits cerebral vasodilation and helps alleviate migraines; also enhances the effect of painkillers and has a stimulating effect.

EVE

EVE is an analgesic brand that Japan's SS Pharmaceutical has sold since 1985, aimed at chronic and acute pain: headaches, menstrual pain, joint discomfort. It's one of the common over-the-counter painkillers on the shelf, works fast, and comes in several formulations built around different ingredient combinations.

Certain EVE products (such as EVE A and EVE Quick) contain the ingredient Allylisopropylacetylurea, a substance legally permitted in Japan with analgesic and central nervous system depressant properties. However, according to South Korea's Narcotics Control Act Enforcement Decree, this ingredient is classified as a psychotropic substance, making its importation or possession strictly prohibited.

Effective April 2025, South Korea will impose a complete ban on importing or bringing any medications containing Allylisopropylacetylurea into the country. Violators will have their items confiscated and destroyed by customs officials and will incur a record of the violation.

    2. Contains niacinamide (B₃), which helps maintain normal skin and mucous membrane function, and provides support for those with recurring angular cheilitis, dull skin, or skin fatigue.

Vitamin B Complex Key Functions

  1. Vitamin B₁ (Thiamine or its derivatives): Improves energy metabolism and helps alleviate fatigue.
  2. Vitamin B₂ (Riboflavin): Maintains skin, mucous membrane, and eye health, and participates in energy metabolism. Suitable for dry skin, angular cheilitis, and similar conditions.
  3. Vitamin B₃ (Niacin / Niacinamide): Maintains the normal function of skin and mucous membranes. Regulates sebum secretion and strengthens the stratum corneum, assisting with acne, dryness, and itching. High doses may cause flushing; it is recommended to take in divided doses or after meals.
  4. Vitamin B₆ (Pyridoxine): Metabolism of proteins and neurotransmitters. The daily upper limit for adults is approximately 25 mg, and long-term daily intake exceeding 100 mg may cause neurotoxicity. It is advised to monitor daily intake limits.
  5. Vitamin B₉ (Folic Acid): Involved in nucleic acid synthesis and cell division, promotes red blood cell production, and reduces the risk of megaloblastic anemia; especially important for pregnant women, as it helps fetal neural tube development.
  6. Vitamin B₁₂ (Cobalamin): Red blood cell production and nerve cell protection.

If you are particularly concerned about fatigue, neuropathic pain, or require high-intensity metabolic support, consider products with higher doses of B₁, B₆, and B₁₂. If your focus is on skin and mucous membrane care, look for formulas containing B₂ (especially the phosphate ester form) and B₃.

Eczema / Atopic Dermatitis Medication

Common Eczema / Atopic Dermatitis Medication Ingredients

  1. Anti-itch

    • クロタミトン / Crotamiton: Has anti-itch properties, commonly used to relieve itching associated with eczema, dermatitis, etc. Side effects may include local irritation or mild redness and swelling in some cases.

  2. Local Anesthesia

    • リドカイン / Lidocaine: A local anesthetic that can inhibit nerve conduction, providing rapid relief from pain and itching. Side effects may include local skin reactions or a brief tingling sensation.

  3. Antihistamine

    • ジフェンヒドラミン / Diphenhydramine: An antihistamine used to relieve skin symptoms caused by allergic reactions, such as redness, itching, and hives. Side effects may include local dryness or mild stinging.

  4. Antioxidant

    • トコフェロール酢酸エステル / Tocopherol Acetate: An antioxidant that promotes skin repair and protects against dryness and roughness. Side effects are rare but may include an oily or greasy feeling on the skin.

  5. Anti-inflammatory

    • グリチルレチン酸 / Glycyrrhetinic Acid: A steroid-like anti-inflammatory that soothes redness and sensitivity. Rare cases may experience mild stinging.
    • グリチルリチン酸一アンモニウム / Ammonium Glycyrrhizinate: Anti-inflammatory that helps relieve dryness and itching. Side effects are rare, but mild allergic reactions may occur.

  6. Moisturizing and Keratolytic

    • Urea: Used for moisturizing and softening keratin, effective in treating dryness and hyperkeratosis. Side effects may include mild local stinging or a burning sensation.

  7. Vitamins

    • レチノール / Retinol: Promotes epidermal cell turnover, anti-aging, improves fine lines, and evens skin tone. Side effects may include photosensitivity, dryness, or mild peeling.
    • エルゴカルシフェロール / Ergocalciferol: Regulates skin immunity, provides anti-inflammatory effects, and promotes barrier repair. Side effects are rare but may cause local irritation at high doses.

  8. Antibacterial

    • トリクロカルバン (T.C.C.) / Triclocarban: An antibacterial agent used to inhibit the growth of surface bacteria and reduce the risk of infection. Side effects may include dryness or mild irritation.

  9. Physical Protection

    • 酸化亜鉛 / Zinc Oxide: Provides physical protection, with antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and astringent properties, promoting wound healing. Side effects may include occasional dryness or white residue left on the skin.

Acne / Dark Spot Medication

Common Acne and Dark Spot Medication Ingredients

  1. Anti-inflammatory and Analgesic

    • イブプロフェンピコノール / Ibuprofen Piconol: A derivative of non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, it has anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties. It works primarily by inhibiting the enzyme activity that triggers inflammatory responses in the body, thereby reducing redness and pain. It is commonly used to improve the skin inflammation caused by acne.

  2. Antibacterial

    • イソプロピルメチルフェノール / Isopropyl Methylphenol: Has antibacterial and anti-inflammatory properties, inhibiting the growth of Propionibacterium acnes and reducing sebum production, which lowers the risk of pore blockages. Long-term use at high concentrations may cause mild skin irritation, but overall safety is good.

  3. Repair and Moisturizing

    • アラントイン / Allantoin: Known for promoting cell regeneration, soothing, and moisturizing effects. It softens the stratum corneum, helping to remove dead skin cells and reduce pore blockages.

  4. Whitening and Antioxidant

    • アスコルビン酸（活性型ビタミンC） / Ascorbic Acid (Active Vitamin C): Vitamin C inhibits tyrosinase activity, reducing melanin production and helping to lighten dark spots and improve skin tone. It also has antioxidant properties that promote collagen production.
    • トコフェロール酢酸エステル / Tocopherol Acetate: An antioxidant that promotes blood circulation and improves pigmentation.

  5. Anti-inflammatory and Soothing

    • グリチルリチン酸ジカリウム / Dipotassium Glycyrrhizate: Inhibits the release of inflammatory mediators, reducing redness and soothing sensitive skin. It also has a whitening effect, inhibiting tyrosinase activity to fade dark spots and even skin tone.
