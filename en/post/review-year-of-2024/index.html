Prospect

The process of writing articles takes considerable time for me. Sometimes, even for a seemingly simple concept, I worry it will be unfriendly or hard to follow for beginners, so I start from the very basics (such as how to open the command prompt). Going forward, I plan to reference previous posts more often instead of repeating myself at length.

Mindset and Changes in Work

The year 2024 marked a turning point for me, deeply influencing my work attitude and life direction.

Initially, I was passionate about work and eager to achieve accomplishments. But as I collaborated with colleagues lacking professional skills and the frustration over unfair treatment piled up, I gradually learned to adapt to the environment and focus on building my own value. Through this process, I realized that true achievements come from investing in myself rather than solely contributing to the company. So outside of work, I put more time into personal projects, professional research, and improving my life quality.

For example, I observed others investing in themselves in the following ways:

  • Starting to invest in real estate and renovations to improve living quality.
  • Continuously learning new technologies and applying them to projects.
  • Accepting freelance work to expand knowledge and practical skills.

The one advantage of the company I work for is freedom and flexibility, which lets me keep a stable work output while focusing on personal research and writing notes.

More detailed insights have been shared in the article Poor Scrum Management. Even though team issues remained unresolved throughout the year, I learned to dedicate more energy to areas within my control.

Personal Aspirations

At the beginning of 2024, I started developing personal projects using Node.js, MongoDB, and Elasticsearch, but as the implementation progressed I kept running into the limits of those platforms. After digging into it, I decided by year-end to shift to Deno, PostgreSQL, and Next.js as my new core technology stack.

In the upcoming year, I aim to achieve full-stack integration for the project and build a complete back-end database system. There will be plenty of technical challenges along the way, but I believe they can be worked through one at a time.

By 2025, my goal is to deliver something complete that I can take pride in, and then keep making it better.
